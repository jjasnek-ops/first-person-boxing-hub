## Can you play First Person Boxing on mobile?

The developer lists **Mobile** in the official Roblox experience description, along with PC and Console. That is direct evidence of advertised mobile support. It does not establish compatibility with every Android phone, iPhone, tablet, operating-system version or external controller, and we have not tested a mobile session ourselves.

**Checked:** 15 October 2026. Use the current official listing and the join result on your device to establish access; an older video or an unofficial guide may describe a different version.

## How do you find the correct game on Android or iPhone?

Use the [official First Person Boxing listing](https://www.roblox.com/games/132956556682909/First-Person-Boxing) and check that the creator is **Can You Box?**. The place ID is **132956556682909**. This identity check is useful because generic searches for first-person boxing can also return unrelated mobile games and other Roblox boxing experiences.

1. Open the official link on your phone.
2. Confirm the experience name and creator before joining.
3. Use Roblox's normal join or app-opening flow as offered on your device.
4. If you need to install or update Roblox, use its official website or your device's official app store.
5. Once inside, inspect the current input prompts before starting a player fight.

This site does not distribute an APK, a modified Roblox client or a standalone First Person Boxing installer. No separate download is needed from this wiki.

## What should you check if joining fails?

Separate an access problem from an input problem. If Roblox does not let you join the experience, use the exact error message and the platform's normal support flow. If you can join but cannot use an action, inspect the current input prompts and test it in practice before assuming your phone is unsupported.

| Symptom | First check |
| --- | --- |
| Link opens an unexpected game | Verify the creator and place ID above |
| Roblox asks for an update | Update through the official channel for your device |
| Join is unavailable | Read the current Roblox restriction or error shown |
| An action is hard to trigger | Inspect its current prompt and practice it in isolation |
| The view feels difficult to follow | Try available camera or graphics settings one at a time |
| Connection repeatedly drops | Check the network and Roblox's official service status |

These are general troubleshooting steps, not a guarantee that a particular error has one cause. We have not established minimum hardware requirements or a frame-rate benchmark for this experience.

## How should you practice on a phone?

Begin with a setup that lets you see the opponent clearly and reach the required inputs comfortably. Then use the developer-confirmed AI practice to learn defense and pacing separately. Do not start by assuming the layout, timing or sequence from a keyboard guide maps directly onto touch input.

Our suggested progression is to identify a guard, learn the slip action, then compare short attacks with longer sequences while observing stamina. The [combat guide](/guides/combat/#how-should-you-manage-stamina) explains why tiredness matters and which numerical details remain unverified.
