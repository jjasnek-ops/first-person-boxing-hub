# Competitor analysis

Checked 2026-10-15. https://www.firstpersonboxing.wiki/ is live and has a sitemap. The homepage visibly advertises codes, controls, combat, beginner, mobile, training and further topic variants. Sitemap language prefixes and article URLs are recorded in raw/competitor-sitemap.xml. Registration date was not independently verified; the user's October 14 date remains historical/unverified.

Observed quality issue: homepage code copy says the developer releases codes, while its status cards contain no active code and provide no primary evidence. Its controls article refers readers to the in-game input rather than establishing a full verified key map. Generic combat advice and multiple close variants do not prove actual mechanic testing. This is a limited page inspection, not a blanket finding about every article.

A real Google browser result for first person boxing roblox showed this competitor as the second organic web result after Roblox, on a personalized US-requested search. That observation does not establish rankings for other keywords or all US users.

Differentiation: maintain an evidence ledger, distinguish advertised mechanics from drills, show source dates/scope, withhold invented inputs and numerical claims, and avoid multiplying near-duplicate guides. Candidate exact controls remains the strongest future content gap, conditional on game-specific footage.
